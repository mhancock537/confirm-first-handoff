Before the System: a Confirm-First Family Navigator

For families of a person living with both a mental health condition and substance use. One bounded moment at a time, this card gives the calls to make first, the one thing to confirm by phone before anyone travels, who owns the next step, and when to check back. The goal is to catch your person before homelessness starts, and to hand you a clear map of the systems when it cannot be avoided.

If someone is in immediate danger, call 911 and say it is a mental health emergency. For crisis support right now, call or text 988.

    The four systems, one page

    Every resource above is real and its site was verified live on August 28, 2026. Nothing here is a live feed. Hours change, beds fill, rules shift. The confirm-first rule exists for exactly that reason: call before anyone travels.

    Workflow boundary. One Roof runs Coordinated Entry for the Birmingham area. When homelessness cannot be prevented, that is the front door, and this card routes families toward it and never around it. This tool does not screen, score, rank, or place anyone, and it predicts nothing about any person.

    Why a person owns every handoff. Referral navigation collapses without funded humans. On North Carolina's NCCARE360 platform, referral resolution ran 88 percent with funded navigators and 30 percent without them, on the same software. Evidence base: impact-birmingham.com.

    Your privacy. This page has no forms and no storage. Nothing about your person is typed, saved, or sent anywhere. The summary you copy names a situation type, never a diagnosis and never a name.
