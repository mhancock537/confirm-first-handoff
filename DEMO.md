# Demo walkthrough

The demo walks one synthetic case from selection to a safe handoff. It takes about two
minutes. Everything on screen is fictional and says so.

## Run it

1. Clone or download this repo.
2. Open `index.html` in any browser. Double-clicking the file works. No install, no build
   step, no server. (If your browser blocks Google Fonts offline, the page falls back to
   Georgia and stays fully readable.)

## The walkthrough, exactly

1. Read the black banner at the top. It states the demo is synthetic and capacity is not
   live. It stays on screen the whole time.
2. In the left column, click **SCEN-01, Immediate shade and water**. The scenario is a
   fictional adult sleeping outdoors during a heat advisory.
3. Read the card top to bottom. Five numbered steps:
   - **Step 1** states the situation with dignity. The person is the user, not the problem.
   - **Step 2** gives the safe next step: direct to the nearest listed cooling center.
     The resource carries a **capacity not live** chip.
   - **Step 3** is the confirm gate: whether that cooling center is open today. The card
     says plainly that the tool cannot check this and a person makes the call.
   - **Step 4** names the handoff owner, a frontline responder, with the marker
     **a trained person takes over here**.
   - **Step 5** gives the follow-up window: within 2 hours.
4. Scroll to the handoff summary. Point out the **Copy summary** button is locked, and the
   summary reads **NOT YET CONFIRMED**.
5. In step 3, tick the checkbox: "I reached a person and confirmed the item above." Say out
   loud that in real use this means a phone call happened. In the demo it is a checkbox
   that records nothing and sends nothing.
6. The summary updates to **CONFIRMED by responder over the phone** and the copy button
   unlocks. Click **Copy summary**. The plain-text summary is now on the clipboard, with
   no name, no PII, the not-live label, and the line naming where a trained person takes
   over.
7. Read the footer: the One Roof Coordinated Entry boundary, and the evidence line that
   referral navigation ran 88 percent with funded navigators and 30 percent without, on
   the same platform.

That is the whole point in one gesture: the card will not hand off until a human confirms
by phone. Software carries the checklist. A person carries the decision.

## What the demo does not do

See `LIMITATIONS.md`. Nothing is live, nothing is sent, every resource is fictional.
